| Signal | What it measures | `worse` |
|--------|------------------|---------|
| Predicted risk | The predicted outcome itself (e.g., risk) | `worse="higher"` |
| Prediction error (Brier, log loss) | Accuracy once labels arrive | `worse="higher"` |
| Confidence (`LogitGap`) | How sure the model is | `worse="lower"` |
| Outlier score (atypicality) | How unusual an observation is relative to source | `worse="higher"` |
